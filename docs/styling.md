# Styling & Design System

## Design Tokens

- **Font Family**: Primary body text is Inter; Display headlines use Outfit.
- **Backgrounds**: Deep glassmorphism `#050508` in dark mode, clean `#ffffff` in light mode.
- **Brand Palette**: Indigo (`#6366f1`), Cyan (`#22d3ee`), Purple (`#a855f7`).
- **Semantic Accents**: Emerald (`#10b981`) for savings & positive ROI; Amber (`#f59e0b`) for medium risk/effort; Rose (`#f43f5e`) for bottlenecks & manual steps.

## Utility Classes (`globals.css`)

- `.glass`: Backdrop blur panel with semi-transparent borders.
- `.gradient-text`: Indigo to cyan to purple linear text gradient.
- `.score-ring`: SVG progress circle with stroke-dashoffset transition.
